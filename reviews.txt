LAPTOP REVIEW SUMMARIES

ASUS Vivobook 15:
A balanced budget laptop for students. It is suitable for programming and normal college work. Its integrated graphics limit heavy deep learning workloads.

Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5:
A good student and developer laptop with strong CPU performance and good battery life. Suitable for programming, data science and machine learning.

HP Victus 15:
A performance-oriented laptop with an NVIDIA RTX 3050 GPU. The dedicated GPU makes it more suitable for AI/ML and deep learning than normal integrated-graphics laptops.

ASUS TUF Gaming F15:
A powerful laptop with a strong CPU and NVIDIA GPU. It is suitable for AI development, programming, gaming and moderate deep learning.

Lenovo LOQ 15:
A high-performance laptop with an RTX 4050 GPU and 1TB SSD. It is suitable for demanding AI/ML development and deep learning workloads.

Acer Aspire 7:
An affordable laptop with dedicated NVIDIA graphics. It is suitable for programming and beginner AI/ML workloads.

Dell Inspiron 15:
A practical student laptop with good battery life. It is suitable for programming, SQL and beginner machine learning but not heavy GPU workloads.

MSI Thin 15:
A performance-focused laptop with RTX 4050 graphics. It is suitable for AI/ML development, programming and gaming.
